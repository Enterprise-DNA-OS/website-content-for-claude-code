# Content evidence checks

This is an operational review aid. It cannot decide legal compliance, consent, retention periods or what is accurate. Actor names are operator-entered labels, not verified identities. Sources checked 22 October 2026.

| Check | Basis | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy evidence for personal information | [NZ Privacy Act principle 8](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/8/) requires reasonable steps about accuracy before use or disclosure | A personal-content record needs operator evidence before approval or bundling. Editing clears it. Evidence text alone does not prove accuracy. |
| Purpose and retention review | [NZ Privacy Act principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) limits retention to lawful purposes | Require a stated purpose and operator-chosen review date. Flag overdue decisions, including archived records. No fixed retention term is invented. |
| Required fields | House rule | Validate configured top-level field types and required values. Nested validation is custom work. |
| Independent review | House rule | Reviewer label differs from the last editor. Approval ties to content revision and field-rule version. |
| Review expiry | House rule | Review date must be present and current to approve or bundle. |
| Complete references | House rule | A bundle rejects missing, archived, draft-only and cross-dataset targets. Weak references also require explicit resolution. |
| No automatic publishing | House rule | Bundles and documents are local files. No deployment, email or external update occurs. |

The NZ rules apply where the Privacy Act applies. Australian operators must map their applicable obligations before live use. This base makes no claim to implement the Australian Privacy Principles. Content classification is manual and does not detect all personal information. Other obligations, including copyright, advertising claims and consent for publication, need your own review evidence and custom checks.

Archiving is not deletion. Apply an agreed retention process to originals, imported files, local history, reports and backups. Use a trusted operator and separate business databases. RLS denies untrusted roles but the database owner can bypass it; the free base is not an authenticated editorial portal.
